# ONE RECOMMENDATION · LUV IT · 6 September 2026

## DECISION A · THE COUPON DRAWER

### Verdict: DELETE IT. The owner is right, and he is right for a reason more specific than "it's broken."

Turn OFF `show_on_cart` and `show_on_checkout` in Power Coupons today. Two toggles, reversible in a second, no code, no deploy. Do not CSS-hide the button (it comes back on the next plugin update). Keep the native collapsed «إضافة قسيمة» field exactly as it is.

### The argument that decided it

**Price-unfairness / equity (Oliver & Shor 2003), in its worst-case cell.** Their control group saw *no* code field and beat the empty-field group on all four measures, including stated non-completion (3.88 vs 4.53, three-group ANOVA F=6.20, p=0.002). A coupon affordance is read as evidence that somebody else is paying less. On most stores that cost buys something back, because a code exists that some customers can obtain. **On this store nothing is bought back.** The launch offer is automatic pricing: bundle discounts up to ~21.5%, quantity tiers, free shipping on 4+ piece routines. The only two coupons in the system are an affiliate template and a test coupon. So the drawer produces the unfairness signal with literally zero offsetting benefit. It is not a trade-off. It is a pure cost.

**Prominence-Interpretation (Fogg 2003) is what decides drawer-versus-field.** A defect costs nothing if it is not noticed. A collapsed «إضافة قسيمة» link is invisible to anyone not looking for it. A button reading «شوفي الكوبونات المتاحة» is written in the brand's own warm feminine voice and converts "she might notice" into "she is instructed to notice" — at the payment step — and then hands a failure, in English, to 100% of the women who obey it. That failure lands simultaneously on the three most damaging items Stanford ever measured (a thing that visibly does not work -1.42, an error string -1.26, for scale a company in legal trouble scores -1.38), on Baymard's "website errors or crashes 17%" abandonment line, and on the roughly one in three shoppers CSA found will not tolerate mixed languages.

**Cash on delivery makes the cost asymmetric, and that is the real argument.** In a card store, a suspicion planted at checkout dies at the confirm button because the money moves. Here nothing moves until she is standing at her door with cash and zero switching cost. The drawer's entire function, broken or fixed, is to plant the idea that a lower number existed. Under COD that idea has three days to grow and one moment to act, and Bain and Google measured what that costs in this region: COD deliveries run at roughly twice the cost of prepaid, with returns and failed deliveries at five times global best practice.

### The counter-argument I am overruling, and why

**"A drawer that SHOWS her the codes removes the reason to leave."** This is a real argument and it is the only pro-drawer position with any support at all (Dukaan, March 2023: a designer's blog post, no sample size, no control group, self-reported). I overrule it on one fact: **it is conditional on the drawer being able to show something.** Fixing the plugin here produces an empty list under a heading that promised «الكوبونات المتاحة» — the same unfairness message delivered more politely. There is nothing to populate it with, and the only way to populate it is to invent a public launch code, which would convert this store's automatic pricing into code-based pricing and move it backwards from the endpoint Baymard says only about one site in six reaches (83% of sites do not auto-apply; users read auto-apply as both convenient and trustworthy).

### The affiliate objection is fully answered

Three independent covers, no gaps: the referral link auto-applies the partner code on arrival (which is verbatim the remedy Oliver & Shor themselves recommend in the paper's own discussion); the native collapsed field still works for the woman who heard a code from a friend without a link; and Baymard's testing is explicit that women who hold a code **"will all find the link, as getting their discount is top of mind before they complete the checkout."** Collapsing the affordance loses nobody who came to redeem.

### Honest sizing, because he will be told otherwise

The pooled A/B evidence for removing coupon fields is **+0.5% at p=0.1335, not statistically significant** (GoodUI, 10 tests). The famous +24% is a single ~1,400-visitor test and a 2017 three-test median that collapsed once ten tests existed. **Nobody should promise a lift.** With zero order history and seven days to launch, this is not a testable decision. The case is asymmetry: zero possible upside, real evidenced downside, at the most expensive moment in a COD funnel.

### Two conditions on the removal

1. Before deactivating the plugin entirely, load a real partner referral URL and confirm the code still auto-applies. The affiliate program runs on a **separate** plugin (Coupon Affiliates / `wcusage`, `coupon-affiliate` endpoint at `woo.php:653`), so it should survive, but that is evidence, not proof. If you cannot test it this week, leave the plugin active with the two toggles off — that is enough. **Do not deactivate an active plugin in launch week for cosmetic reasons.**
2. Grep our CSS for orphan rules targeting the Power Coupons button afterwards.

## DECISION B · THE ACCOUNT NUDGE

### The unifying insight, which changes what he should build

**The checkout checkbox is the only path that produces a working account with no new code.** An account created there owns the order natively — WooCommerce's Blocks checkout route calls `set_customer_id()` and `wc_set_customer_auth_cookie()` in the same request, so she is logged in on the spot and her order counts immediately. Every post-purchase path requires linking a guest order to an account that does not own it, and **there is no such linking code in the repo today.** So the answer to "how do I collect as many accounts as possible" is: **make the existing checkbox free to tick, and ask again later only where the ask cannot hurt.** Not: build an endpoint six days from launch.

He has already won the big fight. Guest checkout is on, account creation is optional. That is exactly what every credible source recommends, and Baymard associates a **10-30% conversion benefit** with going from forced to optional. It is banked. There is no evidence-supported way to get more accounts by tightening the checkout.

### Three placements, in ship order

---

**1 · THE CHECKOUT CHECKBOX · a subtraction, not a nudge · ships first**

Page: the WooCommerce Blocks checkout. Moment: while she is filling in her address, above «أكّدي الطلب».
**Requires the setting change below. Do not ship the new label before the setting is ON** — a friendlier label pulling more women into a required password field is worse than the ugly label.

Replace «إنشاء حساب باستخدام Luv it for everyone» with:

```
احفظي عنوانك وطلباتك بحساب Luv it
```

Helper line beneath, only once the setting is on:

```
وما بدها كلمة سر هلق · بيوصلك رابط تعملي فيه كلمة السر وقت ما بدك.
```

**No loyalty copy here. No benefit line. No pre-tick. No visual emphasis.** This is where I contradict the owner's instinct directly, and the reason is mechanical, not stylistic: at checkout she has zero orders, so a fifth-order reward is an abstract promise she discounts to nearly nothing, while the added decision is paid in full at the moment Baymard identifies as causing some shoppers to stop progressing. The same sentence after the order is a fact; here it is a hook.

---

**2 · THE ORDER CONFIRMATION EMAIL · one line, stripped**

Page: the Resend order email, below the order table, below the total, below the «تدفعي عند الاستلام» line. Moment: minutes after the order.
**Suppress entirely if she already ticked the box.** No new code: this is an `email-copy.json` edit.

```
وإشي صغير · افتحي حسابك من هون وبنحفظلك عنوانك ورقمك للمرّة الجاي.
بيوصلك رابط تعملي فيه كلمة السر.
```

This is not an extra touch in the waiting window — it is two lines inside a message she is already receiving, which is why it survives where a separate promo email would not. **No «توصيل», no «مجاني», no «علينا», no price, no number, no club.** Any number that reaches her during the wait reopens the comparison this whole plan exists to close.

---

**3 · THE DELIVERED-ORDER EMAIL · the real ask · highest quality account you will get**

Page: `customer_completed_order`. Moment: after the courier has handed it over and the cash has been collected.
**Gate it on the real handover event from `luvit_d_current_stage()`, not on the `completed` status alone**, and verify the `wc-luvit-shipped` → `completed` transition end to end before launch. If this fires at dispatch, «بنتمنى تكون عجبتك» lands while the box is in the van, minutes before the knock.

```
بنتمنى تكون عجبتك.
ولو حابة، افتحي حسابك وبنحفظلك عنوانك ورقمك، وطلباتك كلها بمكان واحد.
بيوصلك رابط تعملي فيه كلمة السر.

[ افتحي حسابك ]
```

This is the only ask in the journey provably free of doorstep risk, it carries the most earned trust the brand will ever have with a first-time customer, and it arrives at the moment order two becomes thinkable. **Reciprocity after delivered value, not before it.**

### The confirmation page: I am giving that slot to the door, not to an account

The free `woocommerce_thankyou` slot is real, but it is **not** post-commitment under cash on delivery. She has paid nothing, there is no pre-authorization, and refusing is free for her and costs the store two courier legs. That page has one job. Fill it with doorstep insurance, below the existing WhatsApp button, and put no account ask on it in launch week:

```
طلبك وصلنا · رقم [رقم الطلب]

بتدفعي عند الاستلام [المبلغ] د.أ كاش للمندوب، ولا قرش زيادة عليه، وهو نفس المبلغ اللي بالإيميل.

بيوصلك خلال يوم لـيومين · وبنتصل فيكِ قبل ما يطلع المندوب.
ولو بدك تغيّري أي إشي بالطلبية، احكينا على واتساب.
```

Render both numbers through `luvit_pk_price()` (western digits, `dir="ltr"`), never hand-typed.

## DECISION C · `woocommerce_registration_generate_password`

### Verdict: TURN IT ON. But correct the brief first, and read the email before you trust it.

**The factual correction the owner must hear:** since WooCommerce 6.0 (PR #31257) this setting does **not** email her a password. It emails a **set-password link**. The Accounts & Privacy label is outdated; WooCommerce's own issue #31560 says so.

Why on anyway: the required «إنشاء كلمة مرور» field is the entire real cost of the checkbox, and it sits centimetres above «أكّدي الطلب» on the most expensive screen in the funnel. Removing it removes the cost. This is not cosmetic — the 6 September audit records this exact field blocking order 594 with «يرجى إدخال إنشاء كلمة مرور صالح».

**Three conditions, all before launch:**

1. **Open the email, do not just confirm it arrived.** The WordPress new-user notification is a different mail path from order emails. Read the delivered message: Arabic, feminine, "Luv it" sender name, brand template. An English WordPress "Login Details" mail landing in a first-time Jordanian buyer's inbox while she waits for a stranger to come to her door for cash is phishing-shaped, and refusing costs her nothing. If it cannot be branded in six days, suppress it and send your own through Resend.
2. **Fix the shipped copy in the same change.** `email-copy.json` → `email_customer_new_account.additional_content` currently reads «حسابك جاهز، وبتقدري تتابعي طلباتك منه». With the setting ON that is false until she clicks. Replace with: «بعتنالك رابط تختاري فيه كلمة السر، وبعدها بتتابعي طلباتك من حسابك.»
3. **Confirm on the live Blocks checkout that the password field is gone from the DOM, not merely hidden.** A required field she cannot see is a silent submit failure at the confirm button — the worst bug this checkout could ship with.

## WHAT I WOULD NOT DO

**1 · I would not fix and populate the drawer.** Covered above. A working drawer opens empty.

**2 · I would NOT relabel «إضافة قسيمة».** This is the idea that sounded best and died hardest. Two of the three proposals wanted to warm it up («معك كود خصم؟» / «معك كود من وحدة من شريكاتنا؟»), leaning on Oliver & Shor's Table VI (among code-*holders*, "discount" scored 5.31 on fairness vs "coupon" 4.58). Three separate attacks killed it:
- A collapsed field's **label is visible to everyone**. Turning a neutral noun into a question addressed to the reader makes it more noticeable and more self-implicating, which is precisely what the drawer was removed to avoid. The plan's own Fogg argument refutes its own mitigation.
- 5.31 vs 4.58 is **English lexical framing from 2003 US participants**. It is not evidence about «كود خصم» versus «قسيمة» in Jordanian Arabic.
- **It is a margin leak with an expiry date.** `wcusage_field_url_referrals = false` was measured on 6 September: the **coupon code**, not the link, is what pays commission. Partner codes are engineered to spread by screenshot and WhatsApp. Published copy at `woo.php:483` says the partner code **stacks** on top of existing offers. On a 4-piece routine that is bundle discount plus 10% off plus free shipping plus 10% commission. Naming partner codes at the payment step is an invitation into that path, and the footer already links open partner registration on every page. **Leave the label cold and generic. Its blandness is the feature.**

**3 · I would not build the loyalty card, the linking endpoint, or any club sentence this week.** Everything about نادي Luv it collapses under inspection right now:
- **The reward is worth 0.00 JOD to the store's flagship customer.** Snippet 314 (`free-shipping-packages.php`) already zeroes shipping on any routine of 4+ pieces. `luvit_free_ship_loyalty()` fires at priority 20 and replaces an already-free rate with a differently-labelled free rate. `woo.php:1288` admits it in its own comment. A routine buyer's order-5 prize is a renamed line item.
- **The "your first order counts" copy would be false at the moment she reads it.** `luvit_loyalty_state()` returns `done=0` for anyone not logged in, and there is no code anywhere that links a guest order to an account created afterwards. The confirmation page would say her order counted while `/my-account/club/` printed «ولا طلبية». That is Ryan's red line, manufactured at scale.
- **The counter counts unpaid orders.** It counts `wc-processing`, which is what a COD order is at placement. `luvit-delivery.php` records "رفضت الاستلام" as a delivery-stage meta row and never touches order status — grep for `set_status` there returns zero. So a refusal keeps counting until a human cancels it, and when someone does, her counter silently drops.
- **The endowed-progress citation does not transfer at this horizon.** Nunes & Dreze is a car-wash stamp card with a reward weeks away. Skincare repurchase is two to three months, so order five is roughly a year out. A ladder too long to climb is not neutral — it is a slowly ageing broken promise.

**4 · I would not write `set_customer_id()` or `wp_set_auth_cookie()` from the `woocommerce_thankyou` hook.** That slot was deliberately switched off (`LUVIT_UP_ON` false, `packages.php:1089`) because post-purchase mutation of a COD order is unsafe. And the order-received URL is a bearer token that lives in a forwardable email — logging someone in from it hands her address, phone and order history to anyone holding the link.

**5 · I would not run the blanket `wc_update_new_customer_past_orders` sweep.** Registration auto-logs-in without verifying the email; a blanket sweep lets anyone who registers with an email that has guest orders behind it inherit a stranger's history.

**6 · I would not rebuild the savings line.** `LUVIT_SAVINGS` (`woo.php:1338`) has rendered «وفّرتِ» on cart and checkout since 4 September. Verify it actually renders on a discounted routine (it hides itself when `regular_price` equals `price`) instead of building a second copy in a different numeral system.

**7 · I would put no account or loyalty prompt anywhere before purchase.** A first-time visitor who has never bought once has no reason to imagine five orders, so a fifth-order reward reads as a hook. That is the "cheap advertising" smell the owner is right to worry about.

### Three things that outrank both decisions and should be fixed first

- **The English «The order total changed from ... Please review the updated total» firing at «أكّدي الطلب».** An English message about her total changing, at the instant she commits to handing over cash, is the closest thing on this site to a manufactured refusal. All three angles independently ranked it above the drawer.
- **The top bar promise.** «عرض الإطلاق · خصم على كل روتين وتوصيل مجاني» is not true for singles or for the 13 duos, which are charged 2.50. Name the rule, not the category: **«عرض الإطلاق · خصم على كل روتين وتوصيل مجاني مع أي روتين من أربع قطع وفوق»**. Never promise on the page what the cart cannot pay out.
- **Test order two before launch.** Place a guest order, create the account from it, click nothing, then place a second guest order on the same email through the Blocks checkout. `wc_create_new_customer()` returns an error on an existing email and the Store API turns that into a 400 that **blocks the order at the final button**. Today that is impossible because zero accounts exist. By week two it will happen to exactly the repeat customer the store most wants.

### Copy rules for whoever implements this

Every sentence carries one unambiguously feminine verb (imperative in ـي, or a perfective with an explicit kasra) — unvocalised ـك alone reads masculine. Levantine بن-/من- forms, never Egyptian «بنبعتهولك». «طلبية» not «طلبة». «بتنعدّ» for counting orders, never «محسوبة» (that is this store's *price* word). **Delete «بضغطة» from the project** — if the flow is click plus email plus password, the copy says so. Every «Luv it» inside Arabic prose ships as `<span dir="ltr">Luv it</span>`, and every email is judged from a screenshot of the rendered mail, not from the source. Every number comes from `luvit_pk_price()` or `luvit_loyalty_state()`, never a literal. No em-dash. All copy above is clean on all of these.

## JUDGEMENT CALLS THAT ARE HIS, NOT MINE

1. **The loyalty reward collides with the free-shipping rule.** Free delivery cannot be both the standing promise on every routine page and the thing she earns at order five. Either the reward becomes something a routine buyer does not already have, or the club is explicitly a singles-only programme. **This is a pricing decision and it belongs to the brand owner**, not to Ryan and not to me. Until it is settled, no club sentence should be published anywhere.
2. **Affiliate stacking.** A partner-coded 4-piece routine is roughly 21.5% bundle discount + 10% coupon + free shipping + 10% commission, on a brand whose stated strategy blocks online discounting to protect pharmacy pricing. Nobody has computed the net. **Place one partner-coded order on a routine before launch and put the final number in front of the brand owner.** It exists today with or without either decision in this brief, and it is a bigger commercial question than both of them.
3. **Whether a refused delivery should count toward anything.** `luvit-delivery.php` already knows she refused; the loyalty counter does not. No code path connects the two files.
4. **Accounts versus completed orders in launch week.** I am recommending fewer accounts. The first-month metric is completed orders, not user rows, and the account programme is a month-six asset. If he wants accounts pushed harder than this, that is a legitimate business preference, but he should make it knowing the trade.
5. **Whether five is the right rung count** at a two-to-three-month repurchase cycle. Log median days between order one and order two after launch and decide then, with real data, rather than guessing now.

**And one honest limit on all of it:** not one study cited here was run in Jordan, in Arabic, or under cash on delivery. The direction of these findings will very likely transfer. The magnitude will not. Anyone who hands him a COD-specific or Arabic-market coupon or account conversion number is quoting something fabricated — I checked, and it does not exist. Treat account count as an observed number to log, not a metric to optimise in the first weeks.
